## 3. Leave Policies
### Types of Leave
| Leave Type | Entitlement/Details |
| ---- | ---- |
| Privilege/Annual Leave | 15–21 days/year (accrued monthly); max 10 days can be carried forward to next year |
| Sick Leave | 12 days/year (non-cumulative); medical certificate required for absence over 2 consecutive days |
| Casual Leave | 7 days/year (state-specific); must be used within calendar year, no carry-forward |
| Maternity Leave | 26 weeks paid (first two children); 12 weeks paid (subsequent children); can commence 6 weeks before due date |
| Paternity Leave | 15 days paid leave (company policy); must be taken within 6 months of child’s birth |
| Bereavement Leave | 3–7 days for immediate family; up to 2 days for extended family; HR approval required |
| Compensatory Off (CTO) | Granted for working on public holidays/weekends; must be availed within 30 days |
| Public Holidays | 10–14 days/year (national, state and festival holidays) |
| Leave Without Pay (LWP) | Available after exhausting all paid leave; max 30 days/year; no benefits accrued during LWP |

### Leave Application Process
1. Apply via HRMS/leave portal at least 3 working days in advance (emergencies excepted).
2. Submit original medical certificate within 24 hours of return for sick leave over 2 consecutive days.
3. Inform manager and HR ASAP for emergency leave, followed by supporting documents.
4. Approval required from reporting manager and HR; escalate to department head if manager is unavailable.
5. Real-time leave balance and approval status available on HRMS.
6. Rejected leave requests come with written reasons; employees may appeal to HR.

### Public Holidays Policy
- Annual holiday list is released by December 31 every year, including national, state, festival and optional restricted holidays.
- Employees select restricted holidays based on personal/cultural preference; submissions due by March 31 annually.
- State-specific holidays are observed for local employees.

## 4. Work Hours & Attendance
### Work Hours
- **Standard Schedule 1**: 9 hours/day (includes 1 hour unpaid break), 6 days/week
- **Standard Schedule 2**: 8 hours/day, 5 days/week (role & location dependent)

## 5. Code of Conduct & Workplace Behavior
### Professionalism & Respect
- Treat colleagues, clients and stakeholders with respect and professionalism.
- Maintain a harassment-free, inclusive and psychologically safe workplace.
- Keep company information, client data and proprietary methods strictly confidential.
- Disclose any potential conflict of interest to HR immediately.

### Anti-Discrimination & Equal Opportunity
- Zero tolerance for discrimination based on gender, caste, religion, age, disability, sexual orientation, marital status or other protected traits.
- Equal opportunities for recruitment, promotion, training, compensation and working conditions.
- All discrimination grievances are investigated confidentially and promptly.

### Dress Code
- **Monday–Thursday**: Business casual (collared shirts, formal trousers/skirts, closed-toe shoes; no jeans, t-shirts or casual wear).
- **Fridays & Special Occasions**: Smart casual or ethnic wear allowed.
- **Off-Site/Client Visits**: Business formal attire required.
- Revealing or offensive clothing is prohibited; dress code violations will be addressed by managers.

### Substance Abuse Policy
- Strict ban on possession, use, distribution of drugs/alcohol or being under influence on company premises.
- First violation: Suspension pending investigation; second violation or criminal involvement: Immediate termination + legal action.
- Employees with substance abuse issues may seek confidential support via EAP.

### Harassment & Bullying Prevention
- Zero tolerance for sexual harassment, workplace bullying, intimidation and hostile behavior.
- **ICC (Internal Complaints Committee)**: Established per POSH Act 2013, with internal and external members.
- **Reporting Channels**: In-person, email or anonymous online portal (complaints kept confidential; no retaliation against complainants/witnesses).

### Conflict Resolution Process
1. **Week 1 (Informal Resolution)**: Resolve issues directly with the involved party; document the conversation.
2. **Week 2 (Manager Intervention)**: Escalate to reporting manager if unresolved for mediation.
3. **Week 3 (HR Escalation)**: Submit formal written complaint to HR/Grievance Redressal Officer with evidence.
4. **Formal Investigation (2–4 weeks)**: HR/ICC conducts impartial investigation.
5. **Resolution (30 days from receipt)**: Written decision and corrective actions issued.
6. **Appeal**: Appeal to Chief HR Officer within 10 days if dissatisfied with the result.

## 6. Health & Safety
### Workplace Safety Guidelines
- All operations comply with Occupational Safety, Health, and Working Conditions Code, 2020.
- Mandatory safety orientation during onboarding and annual refresher training.
- PPE is mandatory in designated areas (server rooms, electrical zones); non-compliance leads to disciplinary action.
- Report unsafe conditions, hazards and near-misses to Safety Officer immediately.

### Accident & Injury Reporting
- Report all accidents, injuries, occupational illnesses and near-misses to HR and Safety Officer within 24 hours.
- Complete incident reports with cause, injuries and witness details.
- Company covers all medical treatment costs; first aid kits and emergency contacts available across offices.
- Formal investigation launched for serious incidents (hospitalization, lost-time injuries).

### Mental Health & Well-being Support
- EAP provides 24/7 confidential counseling for employees and immediate family members.
- Regular wellness workshops, weekly yoga & meditation sessions.
- **Mental Health Days**: Max 2 days/quarter allowed; no medical certificate required (inform manager in advance).
- Managers receive training to identify mental health concerns and refer employees to support resources.

### Emergency Procedures
- Evacuation plans posted at exits and common areas; assembly points clearly marked.
- Quarterly mandatory fire drills (records maintained by Safety Officer).
- 24/7 emergency helpline numbers displayed prominently.
- Company-wide business continuity and crisis management protocols updated annually.

## 7. IT & Equipment Policy
### Company-Issued Equipment
| Equipment | Junior/Mid | Senior/Lead | Manager/Director | VP+ |
| ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- | ---- |
| Laptop | Dell/Lenovo (8GB RAM, 256GB SSD) | Dell/HP (16GB RAM, 512GB SSD) | MacBook/Dell (16GB RAM, 512GB SSD) | MacBook Pro (32GB RAM, 1TB SSD) |

| Home Office Setup | Standard | Standard | Premium (standing desk, premium chair) | Premium + concierge setup |

### Acceptable Use Policy
- Company equipment for business use only; minimal personal use permitted (no impact on work).
- Do not share, lend or use equipment for commercial purposes without written approval.
- Lock device screens when away from desk; protect all company confidential data.
- Prohibited access: Malicious websites, gambling, porn and illegal content (violations lead to disciplinary action).
- Mandatory installation of endpoint security tools (antivirus, firewall); non-compliance restricts network access.

### Software Installation Rules
- Only install IT Security approved software; unauthorized software will be remotely uninstalled.
- Use only licensed software; pirated/cracked software is strictly forbidden.
- Open-source software requires IT Security review before installation.
- Submit software requests via ServiceNow; IT reviews and deploys within 3–5 working days.
- Automatic security patches and updates are mandatory.

### VPN and Remote Access Requirements
- Company-approved Cisco AnyConnect VPN is **mandatory** for all remote network connections.
- VPN access requires MFA (authenticator app + password); hardware tokens for high-security roles.
- Access limited to role-relevant applications/databases; extra access needs manager + IT approval.
- VPN activity is logged; idle sessions auto-disconnect after 30 minutes.

### Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) Policy
- Eligibility: Only non-sensitive roles (Security team approval required).
- Approved devices: iOS (≥14) / Android (≥10); Windows/Mac laptops not eligible for BYOD.
- All BYOD devices must enroll in Microsoft Intune MDM for security management (remote wipe available for lost devices).
- Device rules: Password protected, 5-minute auto-lock, latest security patches.
- Company only wipes work data; personal files remain intact. Employee bears costs for device repair/loss.
- BYOD cannot access production databases or highly sensitive systems.

### Data Backup Responsibilities
- Critical company data is auto-backed up daily to AWS S3 (RTO: 4 hours).
- Employees must back up personal work files to company cloud drives (Google Drive/OneDrive).
- Sensitive data cannot be stored solely on local drives; use approved cloud storage only.
- Regularly verify backup status; annual recovery testing conducted.

### IT Support & Ticket Process (ServiceNow)
- Portal: `serviceportal.finsolvtech.com` (VPN required for remote access)
- **Ticket Priority & SLA**
  - Critical: System down/security breach → Response 30 mins, Resolution 4 hours
  - High: Major function impaired → Response 2 hours, Resolution 8 hours
  - Medium: Minor issues → Response 8 hours, Resolution 24 hours
  - Low: General queries → Response 24 hours, Resolution 5 working days
- Support Channels: On-site IT Help Desk (9AM–6PM Mon-Fri), Hotline +91-80-6789-0123, Email `support@finsolvtech.com`, Slack #it-support
- Track ticket status in real time via ServiceNow.

### Equipment Return on Exit
- All company assets (laptop, phone, peripherals, ID badges, VPN tokens) must be returned per exit checklist.
- IT securely wipes all data from returned devices.
- Damaged equipment costs will be charged to the employee.
- VPN, email and system access disabled within 24 hours of exit; employee accounts retained for 90 days then deactivated.

## 11. Remote & Hybrid Work Policy
### Hybrid Work Model (Standard)
- Standard arrangement: **3 days in office, 2 days remote** per week (default: Mon/Tue/Wed office; Thu/Fri remote).
- Schedule can be adjusted with manager approval.
- Client-facing teams may be required to work 4 days in office (department head discretion).

### Full-Remote Eligibility Criteria
1. Role has no client-facing duties and no critical on-site infrastructure requirements.
2. Minimum 6 months tenure with satisfactory performance rating; no disciplinary actions in past 12 months.
3. Minimum 1 year continuous service at FinSolve.
4. Direct manager recommendation (output-based performance management).
5. No negative impact on team collaboration and project delivery.
- Approval: HR review; VP+/special cases require CEO approval.
- Full-remote employees must visit office quarterly for company events/meetings.

### Home Office Setup Allowance
- One-time ₹15,000 allowance for hybrid/full-remote employees.
- Allocation cap: Standing desk (₹8,000), ergonomic chair (₹4,000), peripherals (₹2,000), lighting/surge protector (₹1,000).
- Two options: Submit procurement request via HRMS or claim reimbursement for self-purchased items (original bills required).

### Core Hours (10 AM – 4 PM IST, Mandatory)
- All employees (on-site/remote) must be online and responsive during core hours.
- Remote staff need stable internet; available via Slack, email and video call.
- Exceptions (medical/personal emergency) must be logged as leave/CTO in HRMS.
- Repeated unavailability leads to written warning.

### Communication Expectations
- Slack response within **30 minutes** during core hours; urgent messages within 15 minutes.
- Update Slack status to reflect availability.
- Slack for quick/urgent communication; email for formal approvals/documentation.
- Video calls: Join on time, keep camera on (unless exempt).
- After 4 PM IST: No expectation for same-day replies.
- Respect Do Not Disturb (DND) status for colleagues.

### Work From Different City/Country
- Annual limit: **30 days total** per employee.
- Submit request via HRMS at least 2 weeks in advance (manager + HR approval).
- Employee responsible for stable internet (min 10 Mbps download / 5 Mbps upload).
- Align working hours to team core hours for cross-timezone work.
- Work overseas over 30 days requires HR review for visa, tax and labor compliance.
- Usage tracked in HRMS; balance resets every January 1.

## 15. Privacy & Data Security
### Employee Data Privacy Policies
- Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDPA) Act, 2023.
- Personal data collected only for HR, payroll, benefits and legal purposes; consent obtained from employees.
- Data access restricted to authorized HR/Finance/Legal staff only.
- Employees may request personal data access; HR responds within 30 days.
- Data retention: 7 years post-exit (legal/tax compliance); biometric data deleted within 90 days post-exit.

### Information Security Guidelines
#### Password & Access Management
- Strong password (min 12 characters, mixed case/numbers/symbols); reset every 90 days.
- Never share passwords; IT will never request user passwords.
- MFA mandatory for email, VPN and critical systems.

#### Data Handling
- Do not share confidential company/client data via personal apps/email.
- Use only company-approved communication and storage tools.
- Classify data by sensitivity (Public / Internal / Confidential / Restricted).
- Avoid discussing sensitive information in public areas.

#### Device Security
- Auto-lock screen within 5 minutes; enable full-disk encryption on company devices.
- Do not connect company devices to untrusted public WiFi without VPN.

#### Breach Reporting
- Report suspected breaches, phishing and malware to IT Security immediately: `security@finsolvtech.com` / +91-80-6789-0199.
- Do not conduct independent investigation; preserve all evidence.

### Company Commitment to Data Protection
- Annual security audits and third-party penetration testing.
- Mandatory annual data security training.
- Technical controls: Firewalls, DLP, endpoint protection, encryption.
- 24/7 security monitoring and incident response team.

### Handling Sensitive Information
- Mark confidential documents with “CONFIDENTIAL” label/watermark.
- Secure disposal: Shred physical documents; multi-pass data wipe for digital storage.
- Permanently delete sensitive emails; follow incident response process for unauthorized data access.

## 16. Exit Policy
### Resignation Procedures
- Submit formal resignation (written/email) to manager and HR.
- **Notice Period**: Junior/Mid = 30 days; Senior/Lead = 60 days; Manager+ = 90 days.
- Notice period starts after HR acceptance; resignation cannot be withdrawn post-confirmation.
- Fulfill all job duties during notice period; salary & benefits remain unchanged.

### Handover Requirements
- Create detailed handover documents (projects, pending tasks, contacts, access credentials).
- Conduct handover meetings and train replacement staff.
- Return all company assets; update HRMS with handover completion status.
- Finalize pending work and transfer ongoing matters to successor/manager.

### Termination Process
#### Grounds for Termination
- Misconduct, repeated poor performance (post PIP), role redundancy, prolonged incapability, statutory reasons.

#### Procedures
1. HR conducts investigation and collects documentation (performance/misconduct cases).
2. Written termination notice issued (reason, effective date, settlement details).
3. Mandatory confidential exit interview.
4. Transition support and benefit guidance provided.

### Severance Pay
- Eligibility: Only for involuntary termination (redundancy/incapability); no severance for resignation/misconduct.
- Calculation: 15 days salary per year of service (max capped at 90 days).
- Severance is taxable per income tax rules.

### Garden Leave
- Paid leave in lieu of notice period (for senior/sensitive roles, manager + HR discretion).
- Full notice period maximum duration; employee stays on payroll with system access revoked.
- All benefits and PF contributions continue during garden leave.

### Non-Compete Clause
- Restriction period: 6 months post-employment (12 months for senior roles with confidential data/client relationships).
- Prohibited actions: Working for direct competitors, starting competing business, soliciting company clients/staff.
- Clause is part of employment agreement; violations may lead to legal action.

### Reference Letters
- Standard reference: Confirms employment dates, role (no performance comments).
- Manager may provide personal references (voluntary).
- Detailed performance references require employee consent.
- All reference requests processed within 5 working days.

### Post-Exit Benefits (Indian Context)
- **Health Insurance**: Continue group cover for up to 6 months (employee bears premium; opt-in within 10 days of exit).
- PF & Gratuity: Settled per statutory rules.
- **Leave Encashment**: Unused privilege leave = (Balance days × Basic Salary) ÷ 26.
- Pro-rata bonus for the worked period of the financial year.
- Life insurance ends on last working day (convertible to individual policy at employee cost).

### Exit Checklist

- [ ] Laptop, mobile phone and chargers returned & data wiped
- [ ] External storage, ID badges, peripherals, keys/fobs returned
- [ ] Email, VPN and all system access revoked
- [ ] Data backup/transfer completed

#### Finance & Payroll
- [ ] Final payslip verified
- [ ] Salary advances, loans and deductions settled
- [ ] All expense claims cleared
- [ ] Form 16, PF withdrawal/transfer, gratuity & leave encashment processed

#### HR & Legal

## 17. FAQs
1. **What is the WFH/hybrid policy?**
   Standard: 3 days office, 2 days remote weekly (default Thu/Fri remote). Core hours 10AM–4PM IST mandatory availability. Full-remote available for eligible roles per policy criteria.

2. **How to file health insurance claims?**
   Submit original documents/receipts to `hr@finsolvtech.com` within 30 days. Notify HR within 24 hours for cashless hospitalization claims.

3. **Referral bonus details?**
   ₹10,000 (junior) to ₹50,000 (senior/specialized roles). Bonus credited after new hire completes 6-month probation; apply via HRMS Referral Portal.

4. **How to get IT support?**
   Raise ticket on ServiceNow portal, call +91-80-6789-0123, email `support@finsolvtech.com` or use Slack #it-support. Response time follows ticket priority SLA.

5. **Can I apply for salary advance?**
   Yes (max 75% of monthly salary). Apply via HRMS with manager approval; processed within 3 working days, recovered via future payroll. Max one advance per quarter.

6. **Internal department transfers?**
   Eligible after 18 months in current role. Internal openings posted 5 days externally; apply via HRMS (manager + HR approval). Transfers effective within 30 days, tenure remains continuous.

7. **Conference/Training sponsorship?**
   Annual ₹50,000 budget per employee (performance rating “Meets” or higher required). Pre-approval needed; share learnings with team post-event.

8. **Parking & Cab facility?**
   Free office parking (first-come first-served). Free cab for post 10PM work/night shifts (within 15km); ₹10/km charge beyond 15km. Standard conveyance allowance ₹1,200/month for regular commute.

9. **Gym subsidy?**
   ₹2,000/month gym reimbursement (submit receipts via HRMS). Annual full health check-up also covered under wellness program.

10. **Relocation policy?**
    - Junior/Mid: One-time ₹50,000–75,000 allowance
    - Senior/Lead: ₹1,00,000–1,50,000 + house-hunting travel/accommodation (5 days max)
    - Manager+: ₹1,50,000–2,00,000 + full relocation support
    Submit bills within 30 days of joining for reimbursement.

11. **PF & Gratuity if resigning before 5 years?**
    - PF: Employee share fully withdrawable; employer share forfeited if exit within 2 years.
    - Gratuity: Only payable after 5 years continuous service (no payout for voluntary resignation before 5 years).

12. **Can I negotiate salary during annual review?**
    Annual increments are fixed based on performance; no negotiation. Special salary reviews may be requested (supported by market data) for high performers/role changes (requires department head + CFO approval).

13. **Parental leave & return to work?**
    Maternity: 26 weeks (first two children) /12 weeks (subsequent). Paternity: 15 days paid leave. Employees return to original role; flexible work options available post-leave. Dual working couples can take parental leave one at a time.
